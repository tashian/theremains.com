### 50th Anniversary Celebration!

[Buy tickets](https://fairfieldtheatre.org/shows/remains)

Box Office Tel. 203-259-1036 [info@fairfieldtheatre.org](mailto:info@fairfieldtheatre.org)

Established in 2004, Stage One is a 225 seat venue in the heart of downtown Fairfield, Connecticut. Consistently voted the best place to hear live music in the entire region because of the sound quality, the intimacy of the space, and the caliber of the artists. Artists love to perform here and frequently cite Stage One as their absolute favorite venue.

Seats are limited, so get your tickets early! Doors 7:00pm, Showtime 7:45. Special Guests!
