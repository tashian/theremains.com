### 50th Anniversary Celebration!

[Buy Tickets](http://regenttheatre.com/details/the_remains_live_at_the_regent_theatre)

Box Office Hours: Tues-Sat 12pm - 6pm and ONE HOUR BEFORE SHOW  
Tel. 781-646-4TIX  (4849)

Built in 1916 The Regent remains true to its roots as a vibrant vaudeville house! The theatre is proud to bring live performances back to this historic space, with high quality acoustics. The seating is spacious and comfortable and the sight lines are great from every seat in the house! The Remains look forward to performing in this great sounding theatre! Plus Special Guests The Downbeat Five and Cardinal.
